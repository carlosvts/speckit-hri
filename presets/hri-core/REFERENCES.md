## Notes

- Shiwa et al. studied conversational robots (preference peaks at ~1 s response time,
  responses within ~2 s; fillers mitigate longer delays). Treat the numbers as a
  starting point; modality profiles refine budgets.
- Amershi et al. guidelines were validated on GUI products, not embodied systems.
- The Godspeed indices (Bartneck et al. 2009) are widely used but their validity has
  been questioned (Ho & MacDorman 2010).
