## HRI Rules (hri-core)

> These rules supersede the guidance above where they conflict.

- The "Human-Robot Interaction" section of the spec is mandatory. Fill every subsection; if one
  truly does not apply, state why in one line. Never delete HRI subsections.
- Do NOT apply "standard web/mobile app expectations" as a default for timing. Perceived
  responsiveness and pacing (TIM-) must come from the feature description or be marked
  [NEEDS CLARIFICATION].
- Clarification priority for HRI features: safety > privacy/consent > scope > user experience >
  technical details.
- Inferred human states (emotion, attention, intent) are hypotheses: phrase them as estimates
  with confidence, never as facts about the person.
- Keep HRI requirements technology-agnostic, as in the rest of the spec.
